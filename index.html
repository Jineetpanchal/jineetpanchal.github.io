About

Marketing built on real campaigns,
sharpened by systems thinking.

For two years at Doctecq Technologies, a full-service dental and healthcare marketing agency, I ran the day-to-day for a portfolio of client accounts — content, paid ads, SEO, and everything in between. The standout: taking a flagship aesthetic-medicine client from a modest presence to 151K+ Instagram followers through consistent content strategy, reel production, and targeted engagement, while leading a team of seven through the daily production of hundreds of creative assets.

A Post-Graduate Certificate in International Business Management at St. Clair College added a different lens — supply chain, logistics, and operations coursework that now shapes how I think about campaign systems, not just individual posts. Marketing that scales needs the same thing a supply chain does: consistent process, not just good ideas.

Right now I'm building self-initiated branding projects end-to-end — identity, content, and web design — to keep sharpening design and web-presence skills while I look for a Marketing Coordinator, Marketing Associate, or Social Media Coordinator role where that campaign experience can drive real, measurable growth.
